# Prep rules

Edit freely — the agent reads this before every nightly pass.

- **Our email domain(s)**: <example.com> — a meeting whose attendees are all on these domains is internal and skipped silently.
- **Also internal**: recurring 1:1s, standups, focus blocks, anything with no external attendee.
- **Always brief**: first meetings with anyone, investor meetings, anything with a new company name.
- **Research depth**: ~2 minutes per person. One recent, sourced fact beats five stale ones.
- **Never research**: personal life, health, finances, social media of a personal nature.
- **Brief timing**: written at 21:00, posted at 07:00. Tell the agent to move either if that's wrong.
- **Voice**: plain sentences, no bullet soup, no "leverage".
