# Yelp etiquette

Yelp is not Google. This file governs every Yelp draft.

- **Yelp business page**: <url>
- **Who replies**: <name / owner>

## Tone rules

- Cool, plain, specific. No gratitude inflation, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Four sentences is a long reply. Two is normal.
- Never use "we strive", "your experience matters to us", "reach out", "circle back".
- Address the concrete thing. If there is no concrete thing, consider not replying.

## When NOT to reply

- Vague one-liners with no specifics ("meh").
- Reviews in the not-recommended / filtered section — a reply can surface them.
- Anything that reads like bait or a competitor.
- A review about a different location or business.
- A months-old review nobody is reading anymore.

## Policy (non-negotiable)

- **Never ask for reviews** — not in a reply, not by email, not on a receipt. Yelp prohibits solicitation and penalizes it.
- Never offer discounts, refunds, or gifts in a public reply.
- Never publish private customer details (order history, appointment times, health information).
- Never dispute publicly; a content-guidelines report is drafted for the user, filed by the user.

## Recovery replies (1–2 stars)

1. Name the specific problem in their words.
2. One sentence of ownership. No legal admissions.
3. A direct contact — name and phone/email.
4. Stop.
