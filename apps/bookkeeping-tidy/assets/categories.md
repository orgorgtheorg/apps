# Categories

The **only** categories the agent may use. Replace these defaults with your accountant's actual chart of accounts — categorizing into categories they don't use makes more work, not less.

## Income

- Sales
- Service revenue
- Other income

## Cost of goods / direct costs

- Materials
- Subcontractors
- Equipment rental

## Operating expenses

- Payroll
- Payroll taxes
- Rent
- Utilities
- Insurance
- Vehicle & fuel
- Software & subscriptions
- Marketing & advertising
- Professional fees
- Office supplies
- Meals (business)
- Travel
- Bank & merchant fees
- Repairs & maintenance

## Other

- Owner draw
- Transfer between accounts
- **Uncategorized** — never used as an answer; anything that lands here becomes a flag with a real question.

## Receipt rules

- Receipts expected for: anything over $75, all meals, all travel, all equipment.
- Where receipts live: `/workspace/books/receipts/` and email attachments.
- Missing receipts are named individually (merchant, date, amount), never counted.
