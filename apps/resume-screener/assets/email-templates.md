# Email draft templates

Neutral, warm, short. Personalize the bracketed parts from the resume and scorecard — never send a template with brackets left in. All drafts go in the "Screening emails — <Role>" doc, grouped under **Advance**, **Maybe (hold — no email yet)**, **Pass**.

## Advance

> Subject: Next step for the [Role] position at [Company]
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks for applying for the [Role] role — we'd like to talk. Your background in [specific thing from their resume] stood out.
>
> Would a 20-minute phone call this week work? A couple of times that work on our end: [two slots].
>
> [Signature]

(The scorecard's phone-screen question is for the CALL, not the email — carry it into the doc as a note under the draft.)

## Pass

> Subject: Your application for the [Role] position
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thank you for taking the time to apply for the [Role] role. We had a strong pool and won't be moving forward with your application.
>
> We'll keep your resume on file and would be glad to hear from you on future openings that fit your background.
>
> [Signature]

Rules: never state the reason for a pass in the email (the reason lives in the scorecard, for the user). Never promise feedback. Never imply the decision can be appealed.
