## Structure rotation

Drafts must not all look the same. Rotate among:

- Thanks → the specific thing they mentioned → an open door.
- The specific thing first → thanks → nothing else.
- One short line, no sign-off.
- Address a concern → what changed because of it → invitation back.

## Recovery replies (1–2 stars)

- Acknowledge the specific problem in their words.
- Apologize for the experience — never admit legal fault, never discuss liability.
- Move it offline: a name and a direct phone or email.
- No refunds, discounts, or compensation mentioned publicly.
- Keep it under four sentences.
